# Troubleshooting

## Cannot log in
First, use "Forgot password" on the login page; reset links expire after 30 minutes. If your workspace uses single sign-on (SSO), you must log in through your company's SSO button instead of a password. After 5 failed login attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes.

## Two-factor authentication codes not working
Codes from authenticator apps depend on your device's clock. Turn on automatic date and time on your phone and try again. If you lost your device, use one of the 10 backup codes you received when you set up two-factor authentication.

## The AI agent gives outdated answers
The agent only knows what is in your knowledge bases. After you edit a document, click "Re-index" on that knowledge base; re-indexing usually finishes within 2 minutes. Answers will not reflect the change until re-indexing is complete.

## Widget not showing on my website
Check that the MemoryDesk widget snippet is placed just before the closing body tag of your page. Ad blockers and strict Content Security Policy (CSP) settings can block the widget; add the MemoryDesk domain to your CSP allow list.

## Hitting the conversation limit
When you reach your plan's monthly conversation limit, the AI agent stops answering and hands new conversations to a human teammate. You can buy a credit pack or upgrade your plan to restore AI answers right away.
