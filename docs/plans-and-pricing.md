# Plans and Pricing

## Free plan
The Free plan costs $0 and includes 1 seat, 1 knowledge base, and up to 200 AI-answered conversations per month. Conversation history on the Free plan is kept for 30 days.

## Starter plan
The Starter plan costs $12 per month and includes 2 seats, 3 knowledge bases, and up to 2,000 AI-answered conversations per month. Conversation history is kept for 1 year.

## Pro plan
The Pro plan costs $29 per month and includes 5 seats, unlimited knowledge bases, and up to 10,000 AI-answered conversations per month. Pro adds customer memory, conflict detection, and the full audit trail. Extra seats cost $6 per seat per month.

## Enterprise plan
Enterprise pricing is custom and starts at 25 seats. It includes single sign-on (SSO), a 99.9% uptime SLA, and a dedicated success manager. Contact the sales team from the Pricing page to get a quote.

## Annual billing discount
Paying yearly instead of monthly gives a 20% discount on the Starter and Pro plans. The discount does not apply to extra seats added in the middle of a billing year.

## Message credit packs
If you go over your monthly conversation limit, you can buy a credit pack of 1,000 extra conversations for $10. Credit packs expire 90 days after purchase.
