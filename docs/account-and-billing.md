# Account and Billing

## Billing cycle
Monthly plans are billed on the same calendar day each month as the day you first subscribed. If that day does not exist in a month (for example, the 31st), you are billed on the last day of that month.

## Accepted payment methods
MemoryDesk accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. Bank transfer is available only for Enterprise customers paying annually.

## Duplicate or unexpected charges
If you see two charges for the same billing period, one is usually a temporary authorization hold that disappears within 3 business days. If both charges are still there after 3 business days, report it through "Billing > Report a charge issue" in the Help Center and include both transaction IDs.

## Upgrading and downgrading
Upgrades take effect immediately, and you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle, and no credit is given for the current cycle.

## Changing the account owner
Only the current account owner can transfer ownership. Go to "Settings > Workspace > Transfer ownership" and choose a member with the Admin role. The new owner must accept the transfer within 7 days or it is cancelled.

## Deleting your account
Account deletion is permanent. All conversations, knowledge bases, and customer memory are erased after a 30-day grace period, during which the owner can restore the account. Invoices are kept for 7 years for legal reasons.
