# Refund Policy

## Eligibility window
Refunds are available within 14 days of the original purchase date for monthly plans. Annual plans can be refunded within 21 days of purchase. Requests made after these windows are not eligible for a refund.

## How to request a refund
Open the in-app Help Center and choose "Billing > Request a refund". You must include your workspace ID and the invoice number. Refund requests sent through social media or chat apps are not accepted.

## Partial refunds for annual plans
If you cancel an annual plan after the 21-day window, you will not receive a refund, but your plan stays active until the end of the paid year. MemoryDesk does not offer prorated refunds for unused months.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Refunds to PayPal usually arrive within 3 business days.

## Non-refundable items
Add-on message credit packs are non-refundable once any credits from the pack have been used. Fees for custom onboarding sessions are also non-refundable.
